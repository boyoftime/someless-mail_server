When a call is turned down

The response is {"error": "…"}, saying why in the same words as the panel. A call does all it was asked or nothing at all: a mailbox is never half made.

  • 400: refused, like an address at a domain that isn't authenticated, a password your rules don't take, or an address that's already taken.
  • 401: no key, a wrong one, or one that has expired or been deleted.
  • 404: there's no sender, mailbox or alias with that id or address, or no such call.
  • 429: too many wrong keys from your address. Wait the seconds the Retry-After header says, then try again.
