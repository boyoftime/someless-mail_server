Credits

The people behind Someless Mail, and how to help it grow.

All the credit goes to Someless Tricks, who brought this mail server to life.

Someless

Web developer, and the maker of Someless Mail

Every business deserves mail it truly owns: fast, trusted and private, on its own server. That's why I built Someless Mail.

I've been a web developer for more than three years. I build my tools with the help of AI, to move faster as technology grows day by day, and Someless Mail is the biggest of them yet.

It was never easy to make

A mail server has to get countless small things right before the rest of the internet trusts it. Someless Mail brings them all together: a mail engine built from source, DNS checks that know how each provider works, a signing key for every domain, mailboxes and aliases, the webmail, two-factor sign-in, and a panel that makes all of it simple.

Every piece was built, tested, and built again, over many long nights, until it felt effortless to use. That effortlessness was the hardest part. And it isn't finished: more updates will keep coming to make it even more powerful.

A powerful mail server for your business

Someless Mail gives a business of any size, up to the enterprise, mail like the big providers, on a server it owns. Haven't tried it yet? Add your domain, and send your first message in minutes.

Thanks to the Stalwart team

Their open-source mail engine, Stalwart, is the heart of Someless Mail: fast and secure, written in Rust, it sends, receives and keeps every message. Thank you for building it in the open.

Also built with Flask, Pillow, segno, cryptography, dnspython, lottie-web, PixiJS, the Google Sans font, Devicon, and icons from Iconify and Simple Icons.
