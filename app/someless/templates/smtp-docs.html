Good to know

  • Keep the key out of your code. Every example reads it from the SOMELESS_SMTP_KEY environment variable, so it never ends up in a repository or a screenshot.
  • Send from a domain you've authenticated on the Domains page. Other mail servers check it, and may turn away mail from anywhere else.
  • Port {{ port }} starts plain and switches to encryption (STARTTLS) before the login goes out: that's why each example turns TLS on first.
  • "535 Authentication failed" means the login or the key is wrong, or the key has expired. "Connection refused" or a timeout means the mail engine isn't in yet, or the network your code runs on blocks port {{ port }}.
