Help center

Answers to the questions people ask most. Something else? Ask the person who looks after your mail server.

{% call question("I cannot send or receive emails, what should I do?", open=true) %}

First look in the Spam folder: mail from someone new can land there. Mark it as Not spam and mail from them comes to your Inbox after that.

If your storage is full (the bar under your folders says so), nothing new comes in until you delete some mail, or your admin gives you more.

Still nothing? Ask your admin to check the domain's records (MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC) in the Someless Mail panel: it shows which ones are missing.

{% endcall %} {% call question("How can I connect my mailbox to a mail app?") %}

Go to Settings → Connect third-party apps and click View configuration. Your username is your email address, and the password is your mailbox password.

In short: IMAP on {{ server }}, port {{ ports.imap }} (SSL/TLS), or POP3 on port {{ ports.pop3 }}; sending with SMTP on port {{ ports.smtp_ssl }} (SSL/TLS) or {{ ports.smtp_starttls }} (STARTTLS). On an iPhone, iPad or Mac, Download profile there sets it all up for you.

{% endcall %} {% call question("How can I add my calendar and contacts to my phone?") %}

Add a CalDAV and a CardDAV account (on Android, with the free DAVx⁵ app) with the server {{ dav }}, your email address and your mailbox password. Most apps find your calendars and address books by themselves; the exact addresses are in Settings → Connect third-party apps.

{% endcall %} {% call question("How can I change my password?") %}

Go to Settings → Security Center and click Change my password. Type your current password and the new one twice, then Save & log out: the webmail logs you out, and you log in again with the new password. Your mail apps ask for it too.

{% endcall %} {% call question("How can I set up email forwarding?") %}

Go to Settings, find Forwarding, and click Add a forwarding address. Turn Keep an email copy on to keep your mail here as well.

{% endcall %} {% call question("How can I let people know I'm away?") %}

Go to Settings → Auto-reply, switch it on, choose when it starts and ends, and write your message. Each sender gets it once a day while you're away, even with the webmail closed.

{% endcall %} {% call question("How can I sort my mail into folders by itself?") %}

Go to Settings → Filters and click Create a new filter: choose what the mail has (who it's from, words in its subject, and more) and what happens to it (moved to a folder, marked as read, forwarded…). Filters work on new mail as it arrives, in the order of the list.

{% endcall %} {% call question("Are there keyboard shortcuts?") %}

Yes: in Settings, under Profile, click See keyboard shortcuts. In the calendar, the arrow keys go back and forward, T goes to today and C creates an event.
